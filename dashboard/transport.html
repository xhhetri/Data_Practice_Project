What these data can support

    Boundary reconciliation remains unresolved

    A sales-based combustion calculation uses different activity boundaries and factors from the official inventory. This is a diagnostic discrepancy, not a passed inventory validation.

    Annual model: association, not intervention effects

    Whole-year chronological folds prevent training on a later year to estimate an earlier one. The models still receive actual same-year inputs. High pooled R² can hide poor performance in smaller states.

    Errors for your selected jurisdiction

    A previous-year emissions benchmark is reported alongside both models. The scoring API rejects negative and out-of-range inputs, and labels the result as an associative estimate.
